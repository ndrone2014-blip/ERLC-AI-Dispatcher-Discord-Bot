# Privacy Policy: Hive Dispatch | ERLC

**Effective date:** November 27, 2026
**Last updated:** November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information **Hive Dispatch | ERLC** ("Hive Dispatch", "the Bot", "we", "us") collects, how it is used, and the choices you have. It covers the Discord bot and its web dashboard at **pulsedispatchpanel.netlify.app** (together, "the Service").

Hive Dispatch is developed by **Drone Hive Studios** (Developer: @ndrone40). It is a free hobby project with no paid tiers.

By adding the Bot to a server, using its commands, joining a voice channel it is in, or signing in to the dashboard, you agree to this Privacy Policy.

---

## 1. Information we collect

We only collect what the Bot needs to run its features. Depending on which features a server enables, this can include:

### Discord information
- **User data:** your Discord user ID, username, display name, avatar, and the roles you hold in servers where the Bot is present.
- **Server data:** server ID, name, icon, channel and role IDs, and the settings server staff choose for the Bot.
- **Messages and commands:** the commands you run and, where a server has enabled natural-language dispatch, the content of messages sent in the channels the Bot is set up to watch. The Bot uses the Message Content intent only for these features.
- **Member events:** join and leave events, used for logging, welcome and moderation features.

### Voice data (only if a server uses voice dispatch)
- When the Bot is in a voice channel, it can listen to audio so it can respond to radio-style dispatch requests.
- Speech-to-text runs **locally** on the Bot's host computer (using faster-whisper). Audio is not sent to an outside speech service.
- Raw audio is processed in memory and **is not recorded or stored**. The resulting text may be saved as part of a dispatch or incident log.

### Roblox and ER:LC information
- **Roblox account linking:** if you verify, we store your Roblox user ID and username and link them to your Discord ID. Verification uses Roblox's official OAuth sign-in or public Roblox profile lookups. **We never see or store your Roblox password.**
- **ER:LC server data:** for servers that connect their ER:LC private server, we may store in-game player names and IDs, join and leave events, 911 calls, and moderation actions taken through the Bot.

### Community and roleplay data created by server staff or members
Servers can choose to use features that store data such as: officer and unit records, unit statuses, shifts, incidents, BOLOs, reports, applications and their answers, leave-of-absence requests, staff records, moderation cases, warnings and notes, announcements, scheduled messages and audit logs.

### Dashboard information
- When you sign in with Discord, we receive your basic Discord profile (ID, username, avatar) and the list of servers you are in, so we can show the servers you manage.
- We create a sign-in session (stored as a cookie) to keep you logged in. We do not receive your Discord password.
- Dashboard preferences you save.

### Technical and usage data
- Command usage counts, error logs and AI usage counts, used for fixing bugs and keeping the Service stable.

---

## 2. How we use information

We use information only to:
- run Bot features (dispatch, CAD/MDT-style tools, verification, moderation, logging, staff management and more);
- show your server's data on the dashboard to authorized staff;
- keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and spam, and enforce our [Terms of Service](TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md);
- fix bugs and improve features.

**We do not sell, rent, or trade your data.** We do not use it for advertising.

---

## 3. AI features

Some features (such as AI dispatch replies and natural-language recognition) send the **text** of the relevant message or call, along with limited context like unit names or call details, to a third-party AI provider to generate a response. Depending on the server's setup, providers can include OpenRouter, Google Gemini, Groq, Mistral, Cerebras, Cohere, DeepSeek, Together AI and Hugging Face.

- We send only what is needed for the reply. We do not intentionally send your Discord ID, email or other account details.
- These providers handle data under their own privacy policies. Some free AI services may use submitted content to improve their models.
- **Don't put personal or sensitive real-life information into messages handled by the AI.** The Bot is for roleplay.
- AI replies can be wrong. They are for fictional roleplay only.

---

## 4. Who we share information with

We share data only with:
- **Discord**: the Bot runs on Discord's platform ([Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy)).
- **Roblox and the ER:LC API**: to verify accounts and manage connected ER:LC servers ([Roblox Privacy Policy](https://en.help.roblox.com/hc/en-us/articles/115004630823)).
- **AI providers**: as described in Section 3.
- **Netlify**: hosts the dashboard website ([Netlify Privacy Policy](https://www.netlify.com/privacy/)).
- **Staff of your server**: data stored for a server (such as logs, incidents, moderation cases and applications) can be seen by that server's authorized staff through the Bot and dashboard.
- **When required by law**, or to protect the safety of users or the Service.

---

## 5. Where data is stored and how it is protected

- Bot data is stored in a database on the computer that hosts the Bot, which is operated by the developer in the United States.
- Sensitive settings, such as API keys a server provides, are stored encrypted.
- The dashboard uses HTTPS, secure sign-in sessions and permission checks so only authorized staff can see or change a server's data.

No system is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

---

## 6. How long we keep data

- **Server data** is kept while the Bot is in the server. If the Bot is removed, server data may be deleted, or deleted on request (see Section 7).
- **Roblox links** are kept until you unlink your account or ask us to delete them.
- **Logs and usage data** are kept only as long as useful for running and troubleshooting the Service.
- **Voice audio** is never stored.
- **Backups** may keep copies for a short time after deletion before they are overwritten.

---

## 7. Your choices and rights

You can:
- **Unlink** your Roblox account using the Bot's verification commands.
- **Stop using features**: server staff can turn off features such as voice, AI or message-based dispatch.
- **Remove the Bot**: server owners can kick the Bot at any time.
- **Request access or deletion**: email us to get a copy of your data or ask for it to be deleted. We may need to confirm you own the Discord account. Some records, such as moderation actions, are controlled by the server that created them, and we may refer you to that server's staff.

Depending on where you live (for example, the EU/UK under GDPR or California under CCPA/CPRA), you may have extra rights, such as the right to correct your data or object to processing. Contact us to use them.

## 8. Children's privacy

Discord requires users to be at least 13 (or the minimum age in your country). The Service is not meant for anyone below that age, and we do not knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has given us data, contact us and we will delete it.

## 9. Server owner responsibilities

Server owners and staff decide which features are on and what is stored about their members. They are responsible for using the Service in line with Discord's rules and for telling their members how the Bot is used in their server.
